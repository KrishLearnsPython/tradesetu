About TradeSetu

TradeSetu is a tamper-proof supply chain tracking platform for Indian manufacturers, using QR verification and secure hashing to ensure complete transparency from dispatch to delivery.

Features

Tamper-Proof Verification

QR codes and hash matching ensure shipment integrity at every stage.

Multi-Stage Validation

Manufacturer, distributor, storage, and retailer verify shipments step-by-step.

Role-Based Access

Secure dashboards for each stakeholder ensure controlled operations.
